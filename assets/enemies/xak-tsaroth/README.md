# Xak Tsaroth creature candidates

This is a selection set for the post-Cataclysm Xak Tsaroth of the original *DL1: Dragons of Despair*. It covers the ruined city, its immediate swamp, scripted encounters, and the module's swamp random encounters. It does not treat creatures from the approach through Darken Wood or later Dragonlance adventures as residents of Xak Tsaroth.

The 13 rows in `preview.png` contain five transparent 128 x 128 PixelLab PNG candidates each. Files are at `<creature>/pose-1.png` through `pose-5.png`. The poses are independent concepts for selection, not animation frames or runtime sheets. They have not been registered in enemy data. `scripts/build-xak-tsaroth-preview.py` regenerates the contact sheet with Pillow.

## Research and visual references

- Original adventure text and encounter tables: [DL1: Dragons of Despair](https://fliphtml5.com/ycdes/nwqt/basic). Chapters 2-4 locate the city, swamp, lower ruins and dragon lair. Appendix 4 supplies the swamp random encounters.
- Original cover composition showing Khisanth in the ruins: [Clyde Caldwell, *Dragons of Despair*](https://modulosrpg.blogspot.com/2017/01/ilustracoes-classicas-khisanth.html).
- Baaz silhouette and equipment: [Dragonlance Baaz illustration](https://srd.dndtools.org/srd/races/racesDlance.html).
- Bozak bronze scales and magic: [Bozak draconian illustration](https://5e.tools/bestiary/bozak-draconian-dsotdq.html).
- Aghar proportions and scavenged clothing: [Gully Dwarves illustration](https://1eonline.info/d/r/dwarf.gully.htm).
- Catoblepas classic anatomy: [First Edition *Monster Manual* illustration](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/catoblepas-from-the-1st-edition-monster-manual--42643527703816734/).
- Black dragon hatchling anatomy: [D&D Beyond black dragon wyrmling](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16807-black-dragon-wyrmling).

The online images were visual references; no image was copied into this directory. PixelLab generated every candidate. Poses 1-4 for each row came from a four-candidate PixelLab object review, and pose 5 came from PixelLab Pixen. At 128 px, Khisanth's boss scale will need a separate production pass after a candidate is chosen.
