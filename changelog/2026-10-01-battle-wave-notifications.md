# Battle wave notifications

- Added a centered announcement before every enemy wave.
- The announcement counts down from three seconds before enemies spawn. Regular waves show only the wave number in both the announcement and encounter status; boss waves show the boss name beneath a boss-wave label.
- The announcement uses crisp text and a separate countdown over a dark battlefield card.
- Pausing battle also pauses the countdown, and combat remains inactive until the new wave appears.
- Victory and defeat show persistent "DELVE CLEARED!" or red "DEFEATED" overlays with a CONFIRM button before leaving combat.
