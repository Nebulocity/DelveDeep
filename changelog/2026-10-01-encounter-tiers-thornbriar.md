# Encounter tiers and Thornbriar Hollow

- Defined ordinary delve lengths of 6, 10, 16, 24, 34 and 50 waves for Easy through Impossible. Dolmark Den is now Difficult and fills ten waves with its existing enemy groups until more content is authored.
- The Murmuring Abyss displays Unknown and retains its six-wave Void encounter. Hidden Void Manifestation tiers remain undecided.
- Rebuilt Thornbriar Hollow as six Easy waves with the requested Ruffian, Lasher, Hedge Mage and Rongar the Crusher counts and independent dice rolls. Wave five's Ruffian range is interpreted as 6 to 8.
- Renamed the four enemy IDs, in-game names, source portraits, PixelLab asset folders, manifests and runtime sprite paths. Existing source art and generated frames were moved without changing image content.
