# Ranged idle facing

- Ranged adventurers and healers now hold their settled position while their target remains within attack reach. They still retreat from close threats and approach when a target leaves reach.
- Their sprites face the strongest living monster at most once every 1.5 seconds. A retreat can play the walking animation while the character faces the monster; waiting uses idle frames.
- Added movement and sprite tests for settled range, target drift, retreat facing, and Hold facing.
