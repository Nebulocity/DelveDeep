# Slime Cave waves

- Replaced the shared Easy encounter with six Slime Cave waves using only Cave Slimes, Elder Slimes, and the Slime Sovereign.
- Waves one through three roll their Cave Slime additions independently: 4 + 1d4, 4 + 2d4, and 2 + 2d4 with one Elder Slime.
- Waves four through six have fixed groups: 4 Cave Slimes and 2 Elders; 6 Cave Slimes and 3 Elders; then 4 Cave Slimes, 2 Elders, and the Slime Sovereign.
- Updated encounter tests and game-design wave counts. The progression and landing tests and production build passed.
