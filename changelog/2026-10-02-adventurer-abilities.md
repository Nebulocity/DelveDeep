# Adventurer ability progression and hall menus

- Renamed the hall destination to Adventurers and reused the hall interior across its Adventurers, Equipment, Items, and Tactics screens.
- Grouped adventurers by Tank, Healer, Melee DPS, and Ranged DPS. Selection now displays derived stats and worn equipment.
- Added per-adventurer gold purchases for ability unlocks and ranks, with a four-ability battle loadout and save migration for existing profiles.
- Applied equipped ranks to battle ability values when the party is created.
- Validated with the production build and relevant inventory, equipment, class ability, battle behavior, progression, and new ability progression tests.
