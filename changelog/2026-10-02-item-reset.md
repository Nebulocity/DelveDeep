# Item reset and accessory slot

- Cleared the equipment catalog, crafting data, consumables, materials, Void Keys, and item rewards. Old saved item stock is discarded; gold, character progression, world progress, and tactics remain.
- Kept the Blacksmith, Quartermaster, and Items scenes. Their buy, sell, craft, and inventory views show empty states until new items are defined.
- Added an Accessory equipment slot alongside Weapon and Armor in save data, equip logic, character sheets, and the Items screen.
- Removed tonic combat controls and the Prepared Supplies tactic. Players who unlocked that tactic receive its Tactics Point back when their leader save loads.
- Void Portal access now follows map progression without an item gate.
