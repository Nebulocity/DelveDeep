# Thornbriar Hollow pixel environment

Thornbriar Hollow now has a battle environment based on its world-map forest bandit camp. The new pixel-art scene includes twisted trees, a watchtower, red banners, a palisade, tents, and a campfire around an open combat clearing. It uses the existing layered environment projection with foreground roots that can pass in front of units.

The level definition keeps the persistent `thornbriar-hollow` ID, progression requirements, and six-wave encounter data. Forest effects add localized firelight flicker and drifting embers, pausing with combat. The retained Blender scene remains a separate authoring source.

The image was generated with the built-in image tool using the Slime Cave runtime art as a style and composition reference, and the world map and Thornbriar Blender render as subject references. Final prompt: "Transform the Slime Cave composition into a nocturnal Thornbriar forest outlaw camp in crisp chibi fantasy pixel art. Keep a broad empty dirt combat clearing, foreground roots at the lower corners, twisted trees, red banners, watchtower, palisade, tents, supplies, and a fire at the back edge. Match the world map's mood. No characters, UI, grids, text, slime, or cave features."
