# Shared tonic retirement

- Removed the shared Healing Tonic inventory, combat HUD control, and Prepared Supplies tactic.
- Kept the Prepare tactic category for future item and gear preparation abilities.
- Saved leaders who unlocked Prepared Supplies receive one Tactics Point back, and the retired ability is removed from their unlocks and loadout.
- Kept individual three-use potion pack slots. Potion effects and combat use will follow the new item catalog.
