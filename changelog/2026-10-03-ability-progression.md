# Ability progression

- Added six class abilities to each of the thirteen classes, for 133 active abilities.
- Added Assault, Protect, Restore, and Prepare categories, three starter picks per class, and ten rank values.
- Adventurers now earn one skill point per level. Training spends rank-sized points and happiness-adjusted gold at the specified level gates.
- Enabled training in the Adventurer's Hall, retained four equipped slots, and persisted skill points and ranks.
- Added combat handling for new area and single-target healing, shields, reduction, dodge, interception, preparation bonuses, control, and secondary effects.
- Kept existing ability keys and saved ranks. Legacy profiles without skill points receive one point per current level.
