# Alchemist potion packs

- Added Common Health and Mana Potion packs with three uses each. Health restores 30% maximum HP; Mana restores 30% maximum mana and is limited to mana users.
- Added Alchemist buy and sell screens. Each full pack costs 60 Gold; unequipped packs sell for half price scaled by remaining uses.
- Added individual combat POTION controls, per-character cooldowns, saved use counts, and automatic removal of empty packs.
- Kept starting inventories empty and left potion drops and brewing recipes for later work.
