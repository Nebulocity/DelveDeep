# Ordinary Delve wave checkpoints

- Added persistent next-wave checkpoints per ordinary Delve. Old saves with a cleared Delve open its camp.
- Awarded provisional gold, one material, and XP on each first pre-boss wave clear. Replays cannot collect first-clear rewards again.
- Added persistent camp choices on the battlefield at the final pre-boss checkpoint, including return, repeatable farm, and boss choices. Later visits reopen the battlefield camp. Farm clears pay gold/materials and half XP.
- Kept boss completion and Void Portal reward paths separate. Defeat and retreat preserve banked ordinary-wave rewards.
- Added checkpoint tests and ran the maintained progression and battle behavior checks and Vite build.
