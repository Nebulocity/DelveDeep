# Scrolling world map

- Replaced the fixed map with one scrolling nine-area road world. Provisional reusable terrain and road rendering establish exact area crossings while PixelLab tile art remains to be authored.
- Added POI road routing, animated party travel, camera follow, drag panning, a Find Party control, and travel position saves.
- Put The Murmuring Abyss and The Verdant Tear on sequential road gates. Both become eligible after the three ordinary opening Delves are cleared; each road opens after its Portal clear.
- Renamed The Verdant Tear throughout runtime data, art paths, tests, and docs. Existing player saves are not migrated; a new build starts with fresh progress.
